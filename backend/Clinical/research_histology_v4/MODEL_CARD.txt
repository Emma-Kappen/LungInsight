LungInsight Histology Research Model v4
====================================================

Purpose: Research classification of TCGA LUAD vs TCGA LUSC.
Selected model: random_forest
Patients: 1,026 unique TCGA patients.
Features: clinical/demographic tabular predictors; race included.
CT-derived ranker features: unavailable in current TCGA-linked table.
LIDC data: not used.
MSK-CHORD data: not used.

CRITICAL SCIENTIFIC LIMITATION
Labels are derived from TCGA-LUAD versus TCGA-LUSC cohort membership.
Model performance may therefore reflect cohort-specific data collection and missingness patterns rather than histology-specific signals.
This model is research-only and is not clinically validated.
Do not use for diagnosis, screening, treatment, or patient management.
